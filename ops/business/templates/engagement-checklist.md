# Engagement checklist

Store the completed copy privately. Do not commit client-specific information.

## Authorization and scope

- [ ] Authorized client decision maker and Netherwood owner are identified.
- [ ] Problem, desired outcome, in-scope systems and exclusions are written.
- [ ] Hours, charges, expenses, support boundary and change authority are agreed.
- [ ] Data sensitivity, confidentiality and any regulated-data requirements are resolved.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria, rollback responsibility and stop conditions are written.

## Access and baseline

- [ ] Access is individually authorized, least-privileged and time-bounded.
- [ ] No credentials are stored in notes, Git, email attachments or Formspark.
- [ ] Baseline evidence uses the minimum necessary data and approved storage.
- [ ] Existing backups, vendor ownership and recovery path are understood.

## Delivery

- [ ] Every change has an owner, timestamp, test result and rollback path.
- [ ] Passed, failed and not-tested items are recorded separately.
- [ ] Scope changes are approved before work continues.
- [ ] Client operations remain usable or an agreed maintenance window is active.

## Acceptance and closeout

- [ ] Client acceptance or punch list is written and attributable.
- [ ] Deliverables, limitations, monitoring and owner actions are handed over.
- [ ] Access is removed or transferred and verified.
- [ ] Client data and working copies follow the approved retention decision.
- [ ] Invoice status is referenced without storing payment data here.
- [ ] Public naming, quotes, images and case-study permission are separate decisions.
