# Database and software consulting

Netherwood Data Partners is an independent data and software
business. Apps, database tools and connections between systems are its product
focus. Consulting remains available for work agreed around a specific problem.

## Areas of work

- SQL Server performance investigations, recovery reviews, upgrades and reporting.
- Data migration: mapping, cleanup, trial imports, reconciliation and cutover planning.
- Application troubleshooting and technical evidence for software providers.
- Integrations and workflows with error handling and human review where needed.
- Assessments of older applications, their data and dependencies.
- Bounded AI evaluations for tasks such as extraction, summaries and retrieval.

Projects have a defined scope. Scope, fees, availability and responsibilities
are agreed before work begins. Support arrangements do not imply 24/7 coverage.

The client chooses and licenses their software. Imports, exports, access and
vendor restrictions are checked for the project; there is no claim of expertise
in every platform or of a formal vendor partnership. Some historical data may
need an agreed archive instead of migration.

/migration-readiness/. Do not send passwords or private records.
