Assistant Trainer Support

For help with Assistant Trainer, email contact@netherwooddatapartners.com. Include your phone model, app version and the steps you took. Please leave private owner details and horse documents out of your first message.

Start your barn

Tap Add horse, enter the horse’s name and save. Use the Barn Board to record a workout, add paperwork, log a concern or schedule a farrier visit.

Print a report

Select a horse and tap Print horse report. Choose All horses to print the whole barn. Reports list attached filenames. Keep the originals for your records.

Where records live

The local version keeps entries on this device. A second device has a separate barn. Contact support before removing the app if you still need its records.
