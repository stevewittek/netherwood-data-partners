Assistant Trainer Privacy

Assistant Trainer stores your barn records on the device where you enter them. These can include owner and trainer names, horse records, workouts, care notes, paperwork, photos and videos.

How records are used

The local version uses these records to display the barn board, show a horse’s history and prepare the reports you choose to print. It does not require a user account or transmit barn records to Netherwood Data Partners. Device backups and print destinations depend on your system settings. Files you share outside the app are handled by the destination you choose.

Retention and removal

Records remain in local app storage. Removing app data can erase them. Keep copies of important original paperwork and any reports you need. Copies you have already printed, shared or backed up must be managed separately.
