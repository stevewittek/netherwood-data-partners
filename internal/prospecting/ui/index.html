    Facts, inferences and opportunities

    FACT is a sourced observation. INFERENCE is an unverified interpretation. OPPORTUNITY is a potential service fit. Only explicit, sourced facts contribute to scoring; a company's age or an old-looking website never proves an old internal system.

    Evidence-based prioritization

    These are research-priority indicators, not probabilities, confirmed technology inventories, or fee estimates. Signal selection is a human research judgment. Zero means no supporting evidence has been recorded.

    Outreach draft and human review

    Research → qualify → draft → human review → approve. Sending and response tracking integrations are future work. An approval here records review of this exact draft; it does not send anything.

    Passive public research checklist

    Read ordinary publicly accessible company pages, public job postings, announcements, PDFs and forms. Record the URL, date, exact observation and why it matters. Respect website access restrictions and stop at authentication.

    • Company history, location and industry; distinguish facts from estimates.
    • Publicly named ERP, CRM, Access, SQL Server, application, SharePoint or portal references; record their context and date.
    • Public forms, document processes, fax instructions and downloadable PDFs; these do not prove the internal workflow is manual.
    • Public expansion, acquisition or software replacement announcements.
    • CMS or website appearance describe the website only. They do not establish the internal technology stack.

    No port scans, vulnerability tests, credential testing, private record access, internal enumeration or authentication bypass. This app stores references and never fetches or probes them.
