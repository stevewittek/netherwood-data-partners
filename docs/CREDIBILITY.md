# Credibility and claims framework

Owner: founder. Drafted September 25, 2026. This is an internal editing standard, not evidence that a claim has been independently verified.

## Positioning that can guide current copy

> Netherwood Data Partners provides data migration and systems modernization for small and midsize businesses. We help customers understand their existing systems, extract and prepare data, coordinate with the replacement software vendor, test the migration, and validate the result.

> You choose the platform that fits your business. Netherwood helps you move safely from what you have today into that platform.

Avoid absolute promises of no downtime, no data loss, guaranteed savings, or universal platform expertise. The engagement defines feasible controls, acceptance tests, and responsibilities.

## Keep the evidence categories distinct

| Category | Suitable wording | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Netherwood client work | “Netherwood assisted [approved client] with [verified scope].” | Delivery records and client publication approval |
| Founder experience | “Prior to founding Netherwood, the founder worked on…” | Resume/project history and confidentiality check |
| Individual certification | Exact current credential and holder | Issuer verification and expiration/renewal status |
| Company designation | Exact earned, active designation only | Issuer authorization and current badge rights |
| Additional capacity | Resources may be brought in for a defined project | Confirm availability, role, contract, and client authorization when needed |

## Founder experience draft

The founder's brief states 17+ years of SQL Server/database experience, including production administration, tuning, migrations, HA/DR, backup/recovery, ETL/SSIS, reporting/data movement, Azure SQL, large databases, production application troubleshooting, and systems integration. Verify the duration and supported capabilities against professional history before publishing; do not imply all experience was earned through Netherwood.

Proposed biography after founder verification:

> Netherwood's founder brings more than 17 years of SQL Server and database experience, including production administration, performance tuning, migration planning, backup and recovery, and data integration. That experience informs a practical approach to moving business data and supporting systems that people depend on every day.

Proposed anonymized project example, only after confirming the personal role and confidentiality boundary:

> Prior to founding Netherwood, the founder worked on a project involving an application with a local SQL Server database moving into Azure. This is an example of the founder's previous professional experience, not a Netherwood client engagement.

Do not add employer/client/product names, scale figures, architecture diagrams, dates, or outcomes without authority and evidence. Anonymization must also remove identifying combinations of facts.

Do not publish that name as a partner or imply its backing. Before subcontracting, agree ownership, confidentiality, data access, insurance, client approvals, and support responsibilities.

## Claim register and publication check

For each meaningful public claim, keep a simple private record: exact text, evidence reference, category, permission owner, approval date, review date, and permitted channels. One row per claim is enough; no CRM or scoring is needed.
