# Small-business positioning and discovery plan

Owner direction, September 11, 2026: help local business owners implement
software, take over inherited systems, move data, resolve practical problems
and support staff. SQL Server is Steven's technical depth, not the buyer's
required vocabulary. Retain the existing site structure and visual identity.

## Positioning

**Practical technology help for your small business.**

The site should answer: "I am buying a business or changing software. Who can
help me understand what I have, move the information, make it work and support
the people using it?" Sell a defined review or transition with a clear handover.
Do not imply unlimited help desk coverage, an existing staffed IT company,
guaranteed migration, or a formal subcontractor/vendor partnership.

The homepage leads with software setup, business takeovers, data migration,
system reviews, practical fixes and support. About retains SQL Server and
engineering experience as proof of depth. The inquiry form asks about the
business and desired change, not a technical diagnosis. Technical articles
remain available; their SQL-owned content is not rewritten in a static file.

## Search evidence and limits

This is a qualitative search-intent review, not keyword-volume research.
No Search Console, Google Ads keyword-volume data or conversion history was
available. Search results cannot prove how many people will contact Netherwood.

New Jersey search results show providers describing software implementation,
data migration, training and support, often around a specific platform. Examples:
[Odoo implementation/migration](https://odoovizion.com/odoo-partner-new-jersey/),
[business cloud/software migrations](https://sagesolutionsllc.net/services/cloud/),
[software consulting](https://cedarpointsystems.com/). These are first-party
examples of market language, not endorsements or independently verified claims
about their service quality. Do not copy their prices or performance promises.

Jane's documentation shows an existing vendor import team and a defined
clinic-side export/review process. That supports an offer centered on the
business's preparation, validation and staff handover, rather than claiming to
replace vendor services. [Jane import overview](https://jane.app/guide/how-to-import-data-to-jane-overview).

Google identifies relevance, distance and prominence/popularity as local ranking
factors. Accurate business/service information and genuine reputation matter;
changing a headline alone is not a customer-acquisition system.
[Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).

## Intent hypotheses to test

| Business owner's situation | Candidate wording to test | Useful destination / proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buying an existing office or shop | business technology review; inherited software and data | Defined review, inventory and prioritized next steps |
| Choosing or implementing new software | small business software setup; software implementation help | How setup, testing and staff handover work |
| Leaving an old system | data migration help; move records to new software | Export limits, mapping, validation, fallback and transition case study |
| Staff losing time to workarounds | business software support; fix slow software; reporting help | A measured workflow improvement |
| Needing nearby practical support | business technology consultant + confirmed service area | Accurate location/service information and direct contact |

These are hypotheses, not measured popular queries. Avoid a site-wide emphasis
on generic "IT support" that promises printer repair, security monitoring or
24-hour response beyond the agreed service. Explain specialist coordination
without presenting an informal contact as an established partnership.

1. Complete the consented office pilot in CHIROPRACTIC_PILOT.md. Publish only
   earned, approved evidence and disclose the family connection.
2. Confirm the actual service area and availability. New Jersey is the verified
   founder location; do not create invented town pages or a fake office address.
3. Verify Search Console ownership and sitemap indexing, and the existence and
   eligibility of a Google Business Profile. No profile or account was created
   here. Use real business details when the owner supplies/approves them.
4. Prepare a short referral explanation for existing business contacts and
   software/IT providers. Do not send outreach or announce an Online Computers
   partnership without authorization and agreement.
5. Track inquiry date, self-reported source, business problem, fit, next step,
   proposal and outcome in a protected business record. Do not put customer
   contact details or clinic records in the public repository.
6. Review real inquiries and search queries before expanding pages or buying
   advertising. Measure qualified conversations and accepted work, not merely
   page views. Establish a baseline; no growth result is claimed today.

## What stays private until earned

Clinic identity, patient information, raw screenshots, exact inventory, security
findings and staff quotes remain private unless specifically cleared. The pilot
is not a public client success yet. Rates, response targets and specialist
arrangements must reflect actual capacity and agreed commercial terms.
