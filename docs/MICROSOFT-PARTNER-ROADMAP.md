# Microsoft / Azure partner roadmap

**Current Netherwood status: not verified as a Microsoft partner or designation holder. Do not publish partner claims or badges.** No enrollment, payment, certification booking, or public claim was made by this work.

Official sources checked September 25, 2026. Program names, fees, benefits, credentials, and thresholds change; verify the linked requirements and the actual Partner Center offer before acting. This is a purchasing/skills roadmap, not a promise that Microsoft will provide customers.

## Recommendation for a small consultancy

Complete the business identity and sell useful assessments/migrations first. Free enrollment can make sense once the legal business and domain records are ready. Buy a benefit package only when its usable licenses, credits, or support replace spending Netherwood would otherwise incur. Treat a Solutions Partner designation as a later commercial decision based on real customers and staff capacity.

Maintain vendor-neutral delivery. Neither joining a program nor pursuing Azure qualifications requires recommending Azure when another platform better fits the customer.

## Phase 1 — accurate identity and free enrollment

Microsoft describes [AI Cloud Partner Program enrollment](https://partner.microsoft.com/en-US/partnership) as free. Create the company account through the official process, using a company-controlled work account and an authorized legal contact. Avoid duplicate partner accounts.

Before enrollment:

- [ ] Confirm the exact legal business name, current registration, operating address, domain, and contact information.
- [ ] Confirm who can accept program terms and administer the account; use MFA and a recovery plan.
- [ ] Prepare business/domain evidence privately. Microsoft verifies email, identity, employment, and the business; the account is not fully verified until all required checks pass. Check status under Legal info / Verification Summary. [Verification requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/understand-the-verification-process)
- [ ] Record the resulting partner identifiers and verified program status in the private business record.
- [ ] Review membership benefits and branding rights actually available to that status. Enrollment alone is not a Solutions Partner designation.

## Phase 2 — buy only useful benefits

Published annual USD prices observed on the verification date; recheck checkout, taxes, local availability, and use rights. Product license grants are subject to program terms and are not automatically customer resale/hosting rights.

| Option | Published annual price | Decision for Netherwood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic program enrollment | No enrollment charge | Reasonable once identity verification is ready |
| [Partner Launch Benefits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-sg/partner-center/membership/partner-launch-benefits) | $350, plus applicable taxes | First paid package to evaluate against actual internal use |
| [Partner Success Core Benefits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-success-core-benefits) | $925 | Compare needed software, Azure credits, and support with separate purchases |
| [Partner Success Expanded Benefits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/partner-success-expanded-benefits) | $4,125 | Defer unless business use justifies the cost and larger benefit allocation |
| Solutions Partner designation offer | Verify current fee/selected benefits in Partner Center | Separate qualification/purchase decision; do not assume another package's price applies |
| Exams, renewal effort, specialization audit | Verify current exam/provider and audit charges | Budget time and prerequisites as well as cash |

Use a one-page private comparison: required product, eligible use, normal annual spend, package allocation that will actually be used, activation/expiry terms, and total package cost. Unused seats or credits are not savings. Do not buy a package just to add a badge.

## Phase 3 — relevant individual skills

The closest initial technical credential to the core service is **Microsoft Certified: Azure Database Administrator Associate**, currently associated with **DP-300**. The current [DP-300 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/dp-300) covers Azure SQL and SQL Server management, security, performance, availability, and migration. It also notes annual expiration and free online renewal assessment for relevant certification levels. Confirm the founder's existing credentials and renewal status before choosing a new exam.

Consider Azure Administrator Associate and Azure Solutions Architect Expert only where actual delivery or designation requirements justify them. Confirm current prerequisites, exam availability, accepted certifications, and region-specific exam fees on Microsoft Learn when booking. Do not pursue retired credentials from an old checklist.

An individual credential is evidence of that person's qualification; it does not establish Netherwood's company-level designation.

## Phase 4 — designation fit after real customer work

Microsoft's current customer-facing badge for the Azure pathways is **Solutions Partner for Cloud & AI Platforms**. Data & AI (Azure), Infrastructure (Azure), and Digital & App Innovation (Azure) remain the underlying qualification pathways. The badge names changed in 2026; old Gold/Silver language is obsolete. [Current badge/pathway overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/introduction-to-pcs)

Prioritize investigating the Data & AI and Infrastructure pathways because of database and migration work. The [Azure pathway requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-azure) require at least 70 points and points in each of five metrics: performance, intermediate and advanced skilling, usage growth, and deployments. Microsoft classifies organizations into SMB/Enterprise tracks using customer data; a small consultancy cannot simply declare itself eligible under whichever track is easier.

Before committing time or money:

- [ ] Review actual track and missing metrics in Partner Center.
- [ ] Confirm eligible customer associations and attribution mechanisms with the customer; never request unnecessary privileges just to earn credit.
- [ ] Compare staff certification counts and current qualifying credentials with real capacity.
- [ ] Model the delivery/customer evidence needed, without buying unnecessary customer workloads to meet a threshold.
- [ ] Confirm fee, current benefit terms, renewal requirements, and permitted public wording.

Microsoft's partner capability score is a program requirement only; it is not a reason to add prospect scoring to Netherwood's marketing tool.

## Phase 5 — specialization and reseller choices later

**Infra and Database Migration to Microsoft Azure** is a potentially relevant later specialization. Microsoft's [specialization requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/specializations-apply) call for an aligned Data & AI or Infrastructure designation plus customer consumption, skilling, and audit requirements. For scale context, the current table includes database consumption of $9,000 across at least three customers over three months, with at least $3,000 from SQL MI/SQL DB, as well as separate infrastructure consumption thresholds. Verify the full table, qualifying association types, people counts, and audit terms when evaluating. This is likely premature for a new solo consultancy.

Cloud Solution Provider resale is a separate program with additional verification, support, commercial, and security responsibilities. Do not enroll merely to perform a migration; customers can buy their own platform/licenses or use their existing reseller. Investigate indirect resale only when customer demand and economics justify it. [CSP overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/enroll/csp-overview)
