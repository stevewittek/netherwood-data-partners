# Google Business Profile and customer review workflow

Status: setup plan only. No profile or reviews created. Official policy checked September 25, 2026; recheck at setup.

## Eligibility decision first

Google generally requires **in-person contact with customers** during stated hours and identifies online-only businesses as ineligible. Confirm whether Netherwood actually visits customers or receives customers at an eligible business location. Remote database work alone does not establish eligibility. Keep the decision and evidence privately. [Google eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en)

If Netherwood travels to customers, assess a service-area profile. Use the real operating location for verification and hide the address from customers when there is no customer-facing storefront. Do not invent county offices or use a rented mailing address as a location. A coworking office must meet Google's staffing, signage, and customer-reception conditions. Use one legitimate profile, an accurate service area, the actual business name, and the fewest accurate categories. [Representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)

- [ ] Complete the verification method Google provides; do not buy or fabricate verification evidence.
- [ ] Test website and contact links, calls, and ownership recovery.
- [ ] Review accuracy monthly and after any address, phone, hours, or service change.

## Review request workflow

Use the same neutral milestone for genuine customers: accepted work or an agreed delivery checkpoint. Do not screen customers by expected rating or ask only the happiest ones. Reviews must reflect real experience; Google prohibits incentives and selectively soliciting positive reviews. [Google review policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7400114?hl=en)

1. Confirm that the reviewer has actually received Netherwood services and is not being asked to review an unrelated relationship or former-employer project. Exclude conflicted family/employee reviews. The existing [CHIROPRACTIC_PILOT.md](CHIROPRACTIC_PILOT.md) identifies a family connection; do not use that relationship to obtain a Google review. A factually approved, appropriately disclosed website case study is a separate decision.
2. Once a verified, eligible profile exists, copy its genuine review link. Never invent the URL.
3. Founder manually sends the neutral request below after an appropriate customer milestone. This implementation does not send it.
4. Record request date and whether a follow-up is appropriate; one courteous reminder is enough. Honor any request to stop.
5. Respond professionally without revealing project specifics, patient/client data, access details, or confidential results.
6. Obtain separate written permission before republishing a review as a website testimonial or adding a client logo. A public review is not a blanket logo/case-study release.
